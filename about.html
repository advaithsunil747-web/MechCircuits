About MechCircuits

What is MechCircuits?

MechCircuits is a free online platform providing useful calculators, simulators, and tools related to electronics, electrical and mechanical engineering, circuits, robotics, and other technical applications.

Our tools are designed to simplify calculations, visualize concepts, and help users explore engineering principles without requiring complex software or manual calculations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make useful engineering and technical tools easily accessible to everyone.

We aim to create simple, practical, and easy-to-use tools that can help students, hobbyists, makers, engineers, and anyone interested in technical subjects understand concepts and perform calculations more conveniently.

About the Developer

MechCircuits is independently developed and maintained by Advaith Sunil, a mechanical engineering student with an interest in engineering, electronics, robotics, programming, and technology.

The idea behind MechCircuits came from a simple interest in building useful tools that can make technical calculations and concepts easier to work with. The website is being developed as a personal project, with the goal of gradually bringing together a collection of practical calculators, simulators, and other engineering tools in one place.

As the website grows, new tools and features will be added based on personal projects, learning, and feedback from users.

What We Offer

MechCircuits provides a growing collection of online tools covering different technical areas.

These may include:

  • Engineering calculators
  • Electronics and electrical calculators
  • Mechanical engineering calculators
  • Circuit-related tools
  • Engineering simulators and visualizations
  • Robotics-related tools
  • Other technical and educational resources

We continuously work on developing new tools and improving existing ones based on user needs and feedback.

Who Can Use MechCircuits?

MechCircuits is designed for a wide range of users, including:

  • Students learning engineering and technical subjects
  • Engineers and technical professionals
  • Electronics and robotics enthusiasts
  • DIY makers and hobbyists
  • Teachers and educators
  • Anyone interested in learning and exploring engineering concepts

The tools are intended to make technical calculations and concepts easier to explore and understand.

Accuracy and Continuous Improvement

We make reasonable efforts to develop our calculators and simulators using appropriate formulas, methods, and technical principles. However, results may depend on the formulas, assumptions, approximations, and input values used by each tool.

We continuously work to improve the accuracy, functionality, usability, and reliability of our tools. User feedback and reports of potential errors are valuable in identifying areas that may need review or improvement.

For important or real-world engineering applications, users should independently verify results and, where appropriate, consult a qualified professional before relying on them for decisions or designs.

Reporting a Calculator or Simulator Error

If you think a calculator or simulator is giving an incorrect result or isn't working as expected, please include as much of the following information as possible:

  • Name of the calculator or simulator
  • Values or inputs you entered
  • Result you received
  • Result you expected, if known
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem

This will help us understand the problem and investigate it more easily.
